✓ Technical

# Writing Principles

Show.

Don't tell.

Instead of

"I am passionate."

Show

"I've built production systems used across multiple client locations."

---

Specific beats generic.

Wrong

"I build amazing applications."

Correct

"Built a multi-tenant kitchen management platform supporting digital signage, recipes, campaigns, and scheduling."

---

Impact beats responsibility.

Wrong

"Responsible for frontend development."

Correct

"Reduced development time by creating a reusable component library used across six modules."

---

Numbers increase credibility.

Examples

30+

Projects

12+

Technologies

40%

Performance improvement

100+

Components

Whenever possible,

measure impact.

# Writing Rules

Use active voice.

Avoid passive voice.

---

Wrong

"The application was developed."

Correct

"I developed the application."

---

Prefer shorter sentences.

Average

15–20 words.

---

Avoid filler.

Examples

Passionate

Hardworking

Motivated

Creative

Innovative

Dynamic

Results-driven

Unless proven,

don't claim it.
